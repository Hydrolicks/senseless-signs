Senseless

Learn a sign, then try it on Senseless

These are the 49 signs our device recognizes. Each figure replays one of our own recordings, so it shows the sign the way the model learned it.

  1. Put your hands behind your back.
  2. Bring them into view of the camera and make the sign.
  3. Put them behind your back again. The word appears on the screen, and you are ready for the next one.

No sign matches that word. Try another, or clear the search.

Senseless is a final project at Ruppin Academic Center by Asaf Amrani and Gil Ohana, supervised by Dr. Benjamin Gur Solomon. It turns ASL signs and English speech into text on a Raspberry Pi, fully offline.

Green is the signer's left hand, amber the right. The figure faces you, like a teacher; use Mirror to copy it like a reflection.

Tip: watch it twice in slow motion, then sign it in front of the device.
